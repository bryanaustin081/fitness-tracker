Back up your data

Everything — program, workouts, weight and glucose logs, photos, and archived blocks — is below. Save it before switching phones or starting a new meso. Download a file, or copy the text into a note or email to yourself.

Bring in data from another device

Paste a backup copied from your other device. Merge adds anything this device is missing (logged sets, weigh-ins, glucose readings) and keeps everything already here. Use this for phone → desktop. Restore replaces all data on this device with the backup. Use that to set up a fresh device.
